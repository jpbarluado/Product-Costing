Publish to your marketing site

Downloads catalog.json — the public file your website reads. It includes only what customers should see: business name, product names, descriptions, images and prices. It never includes your costs, materials, overhead, margins or expected volumes.

Auto-publish to GitHub (optional)

One click updates your live site — no download, no manual commit. Needs a GitHub token that can write to your repo. It’s stored only in this browser, never in your files or on the public page. Best: use a fine-grained token limited to this one repo, with Contents: Read and write.
